\pard\pardeftab720\sa80\pardirnatural\partightenfactor0
\cf0 Overview\
\pard\pardeftab720\sb240\sa240\pardirnatural\partightenfactor0
\cf0 The objective of this project is to build a 
\f1\b facts-only FAQ assistant
\f0\b0  for mutual fund schemes, using 
\f1\b Groww
\f0\b0  as the reference product context. The assistant will answer 
\f1\b objective, verifiable queries
\f0\b0  related to mutual funds by retrieving information exclusively from 
\f1\b official public sources
\f0\b0 , such as AMC (Asset Management Company) websites, AMFI, and SEBI.\
The system must strictly 
\f1\b avoid providing investment advice, opinions, or recommendations
\f0\b0 . Every response must include a 
\f1\b single, clear source link
\f0\b0  and adhere to defined constraints around clarity, accuracy, and compliance.\
\pard\pardeftab720\pardirnatural\partightenfactor0
